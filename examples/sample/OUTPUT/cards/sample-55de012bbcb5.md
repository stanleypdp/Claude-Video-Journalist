## claim

## author_conclusion

The beacon has a small effect, but not enough to be profitable after costs (@43).

- Coin flip right in 50.4%, edge of 3.8 points @18

- Cost per trade: 0.04R (on-screen "Cost per trade: 0.04R") @33
