# Does the Zorbex beacon signal really predict the next move?

The author asks whether the Zorbex beacon signal really predicts the next move. He says he tested it on the fictional Lumen 40 index.

For every session he also drew a random coin flip at the same time and compared the two across 500 simulated runs.

According to the author, the beacon was right in 54.2% of sessions and the coin flip in 50.4%, a small edge of 3.8 points.

When the market opened above the beacon, the author says it was right in 58% of cases, against 49.6% for the coin flip.

He then traded the signal: 812 trades over 5 years, plus 7.4R before costs. The author says that after costs most of the edge disappeared.

His conclusion: the beacon has a small effect, but not enough to be profitable after costs.
